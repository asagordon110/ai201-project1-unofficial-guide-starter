Source: Personal UTSA CS Course Difficulty Notes

Students often say software engineering classes are different from earlier programming classes because the focus shifts from writing code alone to building with a team. Group projects can be useful, but they can also become stressful if teammates communicate poorly or wait too long to contribute. Many students recommend using GitHub from the beginning and making small commits instead of trying to merge everything at the end.

Systems-related courses are commonly described as challenging because they involve lower-level thinking. Students mention that memory, processes, threads, and operating system concepts can feel abstract until they connect them to actual code. Reviewing lecture slides, watching extra explanations, and drawing diagrams can help.

Database courses are usually considered practical and useful. Students say SQL becomes easier with repetition, especially joins, grouping, normalization, and schema design. A common recommendation is to practice queries on small examples before trying to solve larger homework problems.
